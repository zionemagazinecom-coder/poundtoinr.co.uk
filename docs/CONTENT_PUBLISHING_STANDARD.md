# Content Publishing Standard

Apply this checklist to every new article published on PoundToINR.co.uk.

- The article body must contain at least 1,400 words. Aim for depth rather than repetition.
- Use one clear H1 followed by short, natural H2 or H3 headings.
- Keep paragraphs short and adjust their length naturally for readability.
- Write in a professional human voice. Avoid filler, keyword stuffing and robotic wording.
- Research claims using current, genuine sources before writing.
- Add one relevant external link. Prefer Wikipedia when it is an appropriate background source; otherwise use a trusted authoritative source.
- Add useful internal links to the converter and closely related published pages.
- Include a unique SEO title, 140-160 character meta description, excerpt, focus keyword, slug and categories.
- Use a relevant featured image with descriptive alt text. Keep the published image at or below 200 KB.
- Verify word count, links, image rendering, canonical URL, sitemap entry, desktop/mobile layout, tests and production build before publishing.
